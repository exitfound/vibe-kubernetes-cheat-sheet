## workloads-pod-replacement-guarantees

### layout

```
WHAT     The same Pod is lost under five different controllers, and the five answers to what comes
         back are three: a replacement that owes the lost Pod nothing, one bound to something the
         lost Pod had, or nothing at all.
DEVIATES WL.A-03: no Node frame. The five columns are five unrelated owners, and a frame would say
         two of their Pods share a Node. The one Node the card names is named in words.
         A-13: no line is pinned to a slot, every slot is empty on some step. Only spine2 goes to 0,
         on none-at-all, with the DaemonSet Pod (A-14).
         M-12: the-blow lands five watch hops on one beat with one fanTiming duration, the longest
         path's routeDur, and one trunk corner, so the balls ride the trunk as one. Own speeds
         spread them 744ms. Registered in render/motion.test.mjs PACING.
CONTENT  Sources at v1.36: PVC Retention Policy, Handling Pod and Container Failures, DaemonSet,
         CronJob. The fan says the deletion is true of every column, not that every owner saw it. A
         CronJob holds no Pod informer, its Job replaces the Pod: `the CronJob itself` keeps itself.
         `every ordinal behind it waits` is ruled out: a replacement is no scaling step. A claim is
         BOUND, a volume ATTACHED, suffixes five characters, the ReplicaSet hash kept on its Pod.
         Code over doc: the Job page replaces a deleted Pod at once, the code waits out its
         back-off.
```
